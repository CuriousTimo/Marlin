## Specs

This firmware is configured for a Crearibo i3 CR10 MK3s fitted with a BTT SKR V1.4 Turbo and TCM2209:

### Motherboard
* BIGTREETECH SKR V1.4 Turbo (https://github.com/bigtreetech/BIGTREETECH-SKR-V1.3)
* **CPU:** ARM Cortex - M3 series | LPC1769

### LCD Controller
 * BIGTREETECH MINI 12864 v1.0 LCD Screen

### Drivers
 * **X-axis:** BIGTREETECH TCM2209 V1.2
 * **Y-axis:** BIGTREETECH TCM2209 V1.2
 * **Z1-axis:** BIGTREETECH TCM2209 V1.2
 * **Z2-axis:** BIGTREETECH TCM2209 V1.2
 * **E0:** BIGTREETECH TCM2209 V1.2

### Steppers
* **X-axis:** 1.8° Zaribo Nema 17 High-Quality and Silent Stepper Motor
* **Y-axis:** 0.9° Zaribo 0.40 Nm 0.9 Degree High-Quality Stepper Motor
* **Z1-axis:** 1.8° Creality CR10 Motor
* **Z1-axis:** 1.8° Creality CR10 Motor
* **Extruder:** 0.9° Zaribo Cool Wound Extruder Stepper Motor
* **Microstepping:** 1/16

### Hotend
 * 3DSWAY V6 Volcano Titanium/Copper
 * 3DSWAY V6 24V  50W Heater Cartridge
 * 24v 40x40x10 hotend fan
 * 24v 5015 part cooling fan

### Bed
 * FYSETC Magnetic 24v
 * 300 x 300mm

### Probe
 * PINDA V2 CLone TriangleLAB (https://www.prusa3d.com)

### Power Supply
 * Meanwell LRS250-24 24v
